**Events** - a steep trend line (EMA 13 angle) cools off: **COOL** = the angle drops back through 23° (the TL ANGLE entry), **STALL** = after 20°+ the angle drops under 5° (the 'is it over?' moment). Down-trends = the mirror. **PAUSE** = price goes +1 ATR in the trend's direction before 1 ATR against it (within 60 candles; both in the same candle counts as TURN). **TURN** = 1 ATR against first. This is fair: on random prices it is 50/50 no matter what any reading says. **Next 20** = price move in the trend's direction 20 candles later (ATR). Each reading is split into 5 equal groups using the FIRST half of the year's cut points, then checked on the SECOND half. **Spread** = pause % in the best group minus the worst group. A reading that really tells pauses from turns has a big spread in BOTH halves, in the same direction.
