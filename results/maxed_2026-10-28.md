## What breaks it - the break candle (the first one under the level)
**Price stopped** = fear: this candle's low is NOT lower than the last one's (greed: high not higher) - the push ran out. **22-candle extreme rolled off** = price still pushed, but the old high (low) the Vix Fix measures from dropped out of the 22-candle window, which shrinks the number by itself (mechanical). **Other** = anything else. **Reversal candle** = fear: green candle (greed: red). **Closed back** = the close is in the better half of its range (fear: upper, greed: lower). **Volume lower** = break candle volume under the last maxed candle's. **Other side woke up** = the opposite pane rose 20+ points since the last maxed candle. **Next 5 / 10 candles** = move in ATR against the run (the bounce after fear, the pullback after greed), from the break candle's close: median, then % of runs positive.

## Interesting facts (level 100+, all charts, all years)

- **False breaks:** 52% of breaks go back to 100 on the SAME side within 10 candles (median 2 candles later). So the first break is often not the end.
- **The extreme isn't always in:** after the break, price made a NEW low (fear) / high (greed) beyond the maxed run's extreme within 10 candles 64% of the time.
- **Snap off the extreme:** median 2.45 ATR within 10 candles of the break (75% of runs: 3.58, 90%: 4.94).
- **Run size:** a maxed run moves price a median 1.42 ATR from the close before it to its extreme.
- **Whiplash:** the other side maxes (91+) within 20 candles after 40% of breaks.
- **Other side asleep:** in 13% of maxed runs the other side stayed under 10 the whole time.
