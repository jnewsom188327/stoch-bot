Data 2025-10-19 to 2026-10-20, SPY, QQQ, IWM, 1m. DATA ONLY. Runtime 0 min.
**Signals** = TL ANGLE: FLATTEN 23° (a steeper line comes back through 23°) and CROSS UP 23° (the angle rises through 23°); shorts = the mirror. Entry = next open. **Jumps** (ATR of the signal candle): **+1 ATR in 10** = price got 1 ATR your way within 10 minutes (ATR here = a SLOW ATR averaged over ~2 hours, so a few quiet candles can't fake a 'jump'); **+2 ATR in 20** = 2 ATR within 20 minutes; **clean** = +1 ATR before 1 ATR against (within 20); **best move 10** = the biggest move your way in the first 10 minutes. (In brackets: vs RANDOM entries the same way on the same symbol and half-year.) **Vix Fix agreement** at the signal, on the 1m + the last closed 5m + 15m candle: the **trend side** = greed for longs / fear for shorts (the side that is running), the **pullback side** = fear for longs / greed for shorts. **Hot** = trend side 70+ on the 5m and 15m. **H1 / H2** = the +1 ATR jump edge in each half of the year (a real effect is + in both). **Luck check:** on random prices groups of 100-300 signals swung by up to about 10 points by pure chance - so trust only a group with 300+ signals that is clearly better in BOTH halves.
