Data 2020-01-01 to 2026-10-28, charts 1m, 2m, 3m, 5m, regular hours (indicators run on the extended-hours chart like yours). DATA ONLY.
**Colors** - VIX FIX RAINBOW: GREEN = greed avg rising + fear avg falling, RED = the mirror, GRAY = neither. TTM FLOW: GREEN / RED = bars rising / falling 2 in a row (never gray on its own). **BOTH AGREE** = your pane background: green only when both are green, red when both red, gray otherwise.
**Fade** = the color ends. From the close of the first candle after it ends, price is followed until the OPPOSITE color shows (or 16:00). **Still went** = how much further it moved the old way in that time (max high for green, min low for red). **Other way** = how far it went against it. **At the flip** = where price closed when the opposite color showed. **Next 10 candles vs usual** = the FAIR score: from the fade candle's close, how far price kept going the old way after 10 candles (close) and at best, minus what any candle does in 10 candles. About 0 = the fade tells you nothing; + = it really keeps going; - = it really turns. (Still went alone always looks big, because the wait for the opposite color lets price wander both ways.) ATR = chart ATR 14 at that candle; $ = SPY / QQQ dollars. TTM never goes gray, so for TTM alone the fade is the straight flip (green -> red), followed while the new color lasts.
