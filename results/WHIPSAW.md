## What a row is

Fear and greed are vfx_pane on 1-minute bars, 0-100. A max is the level at or above 95, or 91 in the variant. A break is the first bar back below that max after a maxed bar. A break is dropped when another break of the same max sits in the prior 22 bars of the same session. Longs need greed at the break bar at least 60 (or 30, or under 10 for asleep). Shorts mirror that. ge30 includes every ge60 signal. Do not add those rows. The fill is the next bar's open, 1-strike OTM, 0DTE, premium 0.30 to 1.00. Entry debit is the print plus half-spread max(1 cent, 0.5%). The sale is the exit print minus that haircut. Commission is $0.65 a side. hold20 sells the close 20 minutes later. tp15_sl25 is target +15% and stop -25%, stop first, else the 15:55 flat. be_a15 has no stop until the option high is +15% over the entry print, then the stop is that print. mom_with drops a long whose squeeze momentum is lower than the prior bar, and a short whose momentum is higher. range_lt_3atr keeps a break bar whose high-low is under 3 ATR(14). vwap_with keeps a long closing at or above the session VWAP and a short closing at or below it. Filters are separate rows, not stacked. Noon is an entry minute from 12:00 through 12:59. Train is the first 75% of sessions. Held-out is the last 25%. The bootstrap CI is the day-clustered 95% interval of the mean.
